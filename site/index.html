Questions

Is this related to Apple's Xcode? +
No. Xcode by DataDropX is an independent editor. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Apple, and it does not build iOS/macOS apps.
How much memory does it use? +
Around 150–230 MB idle on a typical machine, versus ~350 MB – 1 GB+ for a full IDE.
Is it free and open source? +
Yes — MIT licensed. Source, releases and issues are on GitHub.
